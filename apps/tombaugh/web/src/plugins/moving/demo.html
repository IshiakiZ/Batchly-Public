Everything on this page is synthetic: a fake ephemeris service, a fake exposure index and fake cutouts with a moving body over a fixed star field. Names are borrowed from real bodies so the catalogue can be tried; the numbers are not theirs. Try Pluto (bright, methane bands), Eris (needs the stack), Sedna (not detected), Ceres (saturates), 3I/ATLAS (a coma and CO2), Gonggong (never observed), or type "ATLAS" or "Nibiru" for the error states.
