Validation plugin, on its own

This page runs the panel against a fake app and made-up frames with a known answer. Nothing here is SPHEREx data. The frames are 6.15 arcsec pixels with their own roll, pointing and wavelength, pushed through the real reprojection, so the numbers on the right come from the same code the app runs.

The fake archive answers for four of the five challenge targets. For WISE 0855-0714 it lists no exposures, so the report has to say so. Add ?slow=1 to the address to make one load hang and see the timeout.
