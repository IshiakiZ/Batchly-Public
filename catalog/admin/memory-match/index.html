HOW TO PLAY

FIND EVERY MATCHING PAIR

The board starts face down. Flip two cards per turn:

  • If the two symbols match, they stay face up.
  • If they don't, both flip back; remember where they were.
  • Clear the whole board to win. 4×4 is 8 pairs, 6×6 is 18.
  • Every symbol has its own shape and colour, so a pair is always tellable apart even if two colours look alike.

Controls: tap / click a card to flip it. ←↑↓→ move focus · Enter/Space flip · N new board · Esc close.

There is no leaderboard here: a good game means fewer turns and less time, which is lower-is-better and would rank backwards on a highest-score board. Your fastest and fewest-turn runs are kept on this device only.
