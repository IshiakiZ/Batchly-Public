Direction

Text is encoded through its UTF-8 bytes, so accents, CJK and emoji survive intact. Decoding accepts standard and URL-safe alphabets, with or without padding, and ignores line breaks.

Encoding escapes & < > " ', the five characters that can break out of HTML text or an attribute. Decoding understands every named reference the browser knows (&nbsp;, &hellip;, …) plus numeric and hex forms.

Hex here means the UTF-8 bytes of the text, two digits per byte. Decoding ignores whitespace, :, ,, - and 0x / \x prefixes.

Decode only: the signature is never checked This tool splits the token and base64url-decodes it. It does not verify the signature, the issuer, or the audience. A token that parses cleanly here can still be forged, tampered with, or expired. Never make a trust decision from what you see below; only your server, holding the key, can say a token is genuine.
Don't paste production tokens A JWT payload is not encrypted; it is readable by anyone who has the string, and the token itself is a live credential until it expires. Nothing leaves this page, but the safest habit is to use expired or test tokens here.
