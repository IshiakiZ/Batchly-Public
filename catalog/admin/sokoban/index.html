HOW TO PLAY

PUSH EVERY CRATE ONTO A GOAL

Move the keeper around the warehouse and shove crates onto the marked goal tiles. Clear every goal to finish the level.

  • You can only push (never pull) and only one crate at a time. You can't push a crate into a wall or into another crate.
  • A crate shoved into a corner (off a goal) is stuck for good. If that happens, Undo or Reset.
  • PAR is the fewest pushes that can possibly clear the board: an exact optimum. Matching it is a perfect solve.
  • Undo has full history; Redo steps you back forward.

Controls: ←↑↓→ or WASD to move · the on-screen D-pad (hold to repeat) · swipe the board · or tap a tile next to the keeper to step.

Keys: U/Ctrl+Z undo · Y redo · R reset · L levels · Esc close.

Every level was verified solvable by an external push-optimal solver before shipping, and the PAR shown is that solver's exact minimum, never a guess.
