HOW TO PLAY

OUTFLANK. FLIP. OWN THE BOARD.

You and the computer take turns placing discs. A move is legal only if it flanks a straight line of the opponent's discs (horizontal, vertical or diagonal) that ends in one of your discs. Every flanked disc between the two flips to your colour.

  • Legal squares are marked with a dot. Hover or focus one to preview which discs would flip.
  • If you have no legal move you must pass: the game does this for you and tells you.
  • The game ends when neither side can move. Most discs wins.
  • Corners are gold: they can never be flipped back. Beware the squares next to an empty corner; playing there often hands the corner over.
  • Mobility matters: keeping more options than your opponent is usually stronger than grabbing the most discs early.

Difficulty sets how deep the computer thinks. Easy slips up on purpose; Hard searches several moves ahead and solves the endgame exactly.

Keyboard: ←↑↓→ move · Enter/Space place · N new · U undo · H hints · Esc close

This is a game against the computer, so there is no leaderboard and no score is posted.
