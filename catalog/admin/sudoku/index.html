HOW TO PLAY

Fill every empty cell so each row, each column and each 3×3 box contains the digits 1–9 exactly once. Every puzzle here is generated and then proven to have exactly one solution: there is always a single right answer, and HINT can always give you the next one.

CONTROLS

click / tap: select a cell

1–9: enter a digit

0 Del ⌫: erase

←↑→↓: move selection

N: notes mode on/off

H: hint

U / Ctrl+Z: undo

C: check board

P: pause

Esc: deselect / close

NOTES

Turn on NOTES and digits are written as small pencil marks instead of answers. Typing the same mark again removes it. Placing a real digit automatically rubs that pencil mark out of every cell it can see. Re-typing a digit already in the selected cell clears it.

MISTAKES

With MISTAKES on, a wrong digit turns red the moment you place it. Switch it off for a pure solve with no feedback at all; then use CHECK whenever you want the board audited. Your mistake count is tracked either way.

HINTS

HINT fills the selected cell (or finds the easiest cell on the board) and tells you the actual logic behind it. When no clean deduction exists yet (common on EXPERT, or once a wrong digit has poisoned the board) it reveals the digit and says so rather than inventing a reason. Revealed cells lock in gold.

DIFFICULTY

Puzzles are rated by the hardest technique they actually force you to use: the generator solves each one the way a person would and keeps digging until the rating lands in the band, so the label means something. EASY is singles only. MEDIUM adds hidden singles. HARD needs locked candidates or pairs. EXPERT needs triples, X-wings or chains. The panel shows each puzzle's rating under NEEDS.

NO LEADERBOARD

Nothing is submitted anywhere. The clock is for you. Take as long as you like.
